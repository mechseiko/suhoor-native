How Suhoor Works:


Choose to Fast
Everyday, Suhoor asks whether you want to fast:

Yes: Your Suhoor alarm will be active during your wake-up window. To dismiss this alarm, you will need to input your personal 4-digit wake-up PIN. After your alarm goes off, your group can help wake you if you have not checked in.

No: Your Suhoor alarm will not ring, and your group members will not be able to buzz you.

Default Preference: If you forget to make a choice, Suhoor uses your fasting preferences in your settings for special fasting days such as Mondays, Thursdays, White Days, and Ramadan, defaulting to "No" for normal days.

Your Alarm Rings

If you choose to fast, your wake-up window begins at your chosen wake-up time (a set number of minutes before the end of Suhoor), which is when your alarm rings.

By default, Suhoor starts your wake-up window 45 minutes before Suhoor ends.

You Check In

Once you are awake, open the Groups tab and tap I'M AWAKE.

Checking in tells everyone in your groups: "I'm awake. You don't need to worry about me."

Once you check in, other group members can no longer buzz you for that wake-up window.

Your Group Helps
If you chose to fast but have not checked in, members of your groups can see that you are still asleep.

You choose to fast.

Your wake-up window begins.

You still haven't checked in.

Your group can buzz you to help wake you up.

How Buzzing Works
You can help wake another group member when they:

Chose to fast

Are within their wake-up window

Have not checked in yet

When you buzz someone, their phone alerts them that a member of their group is trying to wake them for Suhoor. If they are already being buzzed by another person in your group, you will be notified and must wait until the 2-minute cooldown time ends. Once they check in, the buzz option disappears.

Your Fasting Journey

When you choose to fast and successfully check in during your wake-up window, Suhoor records that day as part of your fasting journey.

Total Days Fasted: Your progress is based on the total number of days you have fasted.

There are no fasting streaks because Muslims are not expected to fast every day.

As your fasting journey grows, you can unlock milestones and badges based on your activity.

Use the Mobile App

The full Suhoor wake-up experience happens on the mobile app, where your alarms, notifications, check-ins, and group wake-up features work together.

Android: Available via Google Play Store.

iOS: Suhoor for iOS is currently in development.
